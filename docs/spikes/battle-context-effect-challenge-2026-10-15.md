# Battle-effect challenge, 2026-10-15

The ordinary-battle numeric residual improved held-out target selection from 60% to 96%, but the latest eight independent openings produced identical 7/8 wins for source and both candidates. The preceding effect-root audit found no arithmetic exception in 26 native branches. Those results do not establish effect reasoning or playing strength.

## Preregistered diagnostic

Freeze the source policy and both final numeric residuals from `battle-context-policy-pilot-2026-10-14` (seeds 2026101452 and 2026101453). No training or parameter selection.

Construct 40 public, face-up boards: eight each for Spirit Reaper battle indestructibility, Amazoness Swords Woman damage reflection, Spear Dragon piercing, Cat’s Ear Tribe damage-step attack modification, and ordinary-monster controls. Each family has two defender variants, both actor seats, and both target orders. The variants and mirrors are correlated, not 40 independent natural games. Native card scripts and database establish fixture expectations; native engine outcomes establish diagnostic labels.

Run both legal target branches at each root (80 total). Repeat both branches of the first two roots in each effect family (16 cold repeats). Compare full root observations and replay actions. End after the first battle has resolved at the next battle command, including continuous post-battle effects. Bound every branch to 80 decisions and the service to 30 minutes.

Labels use only unique strict Pareto dominance in local damage and destruction, conditioned on continuing the chosen attack. Ties/tradeoffs/cancel receive no invented strategic label. Compare source and frozen candidates on conditional greedy accuracy, probability of the native-preferred target, and source-correct/candidate-wrong transitions. Preserve unconditioned action probabilities too.

Coverage requires all 80 branches healthy, all 16 cold repeats identical, all ordinary controls matching arithmetic, and an arithmetic exception in every effect family. Safety requires no newly incorrect greedy choices and no greater than 0.05 mean preferred-probability loss in any effect family for either seed. A candidate gate failure blocks promotion but is a valid research result. Engine/replay/identity/nonfinite failures preserve STOP and end the attempt.

These are constructed passive-effect challenges, not hidden-response coverage, natural prevalence estimates, or a win-rate claim. No large retraining is authorized by passing this test alone.

Evidence: `out/research/battle-context-effect-challenge-2026-10-15/`.

## Results: coverage passed, both candidates rejected

The study completed all 80 branches and 16 exact cold repeats without retries,
unknown/undecodable messages, script errors, or identity changes. All 16 ordinary
control branches matched arithmetic. Each effect family supplied eight genuine
mismatches, for 32 native exceptions total. The report is healthy and complete;
its candidate safety gate is false. Completion is not candidate acceptance.

| Family | Roots | Source correct | Candidate 2026101452 | Candidate 2026101453 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Battle indestructibility | 8 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| Damage reflection | 8 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| Piercing | 8 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Damage-step attack changes | 8 | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| Ordinary controls | 8 | 6 | 8 | 7 |

Across the 32 effect roots, source is correct on 8; both candidates are correct on
zero and turn all eight source-correct choices into errors. Mean probability of
the native-preferred target also falls in every family: source 40.12%, 31.83%,
24.34%, 36.88% versus candidate ranges 0.065–0.119%, 0.00335–0.00355%,
5.55–8.92%, 0.000524–0.000745%, respectively. Every preregistered per-family,
per-seed safety gate fails. Mirrored slots/seats do not make these independent
samples; this is evidence of concrete failure modes, not a population rate.

For example, Blue-Eyes attacking Amazoness Swords Woman incurs 1500 damage to
itself, while attacking Dark Magician deals 500 to the opponent; both destroy the
target and preserve Blue-Eyes. Ignoring reflection predicts 1500 opponent damage
for Swords Woman and reverses the correct target ordering. Cat’s Ear Tribe changes
the attacking Blue-Eyes' base attack to 200 during damage calculation, causing
mutual destruction rather than the apparent easy attack. Native results matched
the independently specified fixture expectations in both cases.

The ordinary-battle improvements remain valid in their measured scope. However,
the frozen numeric residual strongly overrides source preferences on these effect
exceptions. It must not be integrated as an unrestricted actor correction or used
to justify scaling RL. The source also performs poorly, so simply reverting the
residual does not solve effect reasoning.

Next bounded work should learn effect-conditioned corrections from native labels,
retain ordinary-battle controls, and hold out entire effect/card families and new
stats/boards. These challenge roots are now diagnostic/development evidence and
must not be reused as untouched validation. A fallback based only on the monster's
normal/effect type is insufficient: spells/traps can alter ordinary monsters too.
Require an independent effect challenge and behavior retention before a small
retraining canary; no new training was run in this study.
